How it works

From missed call to won customer

No apps for your customers. No tech headaches for you. Here's exactly what happens, start to finish.

1

Someone calls and you can't pick up

You're with a customer, on a job, driving, or it's simply after hours. The call would normally go to voicemail — and most people just hang up and try the next business.

Good to know

The Answer Mate only steps in when you don't answer. Calls you do pick up are completely unaffected.

2

We text the caller back in seconds

The moment a call goes unanswered, The Answer Mate sends the caller a friendly text on your behalf — introducing your business and asking how it can help. It feels personal, never automated.

Behind the scenes

The message goes out in seconds, while the caller is still thinking about you — long before they'd call a competitor.

3

We find out exactly what they need

The Answer Mate has a natural back-and-forth conversation with the caller — getting their name, what they're after, where they are, and when suits them. It keeps things short and friendly, just like you would.

Smart by design

Time-wasters and wrong numbers get politely filtered out, so only real enquiries reach you.

4

You get a ready-to-call lead

A tidy summary lands on your phone — who called, what they want, where they are, and when they're free. When you get a spare minute, you call them back already knowing the job. No phone tag, no guesswork.

The payoff

You win work you'd otherwise never have known about — without stopping what you were doing.
